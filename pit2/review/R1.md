# R1 design review: Figur (Gates of Babylon) @ 54f0c50

Reviewer R1, independent of R2. Method: `vite preview` on :4931, Playwright/Chromium driving the real app at 1280x720, 1440x810 and 1920x1080 (palette open/hover, HTML5 drag of XOR onto the canvas, three wires, switch toggles, a rejected second wire into a taken pin, grid button twice for toasts, 4 switches + 1 lamp added by click, table scrolled vertically and sideways, wheel zoom in/out, node hover + delete, and one keyboard-only pass with 16 Tabs plus Enter-wiring under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`). Every claim below was measured with Pillow on the screenshots or read from `getComputedStyle`; OCR (tesseract 5.3.4, psm 6, 3-4x upscale) on the lettered text. Screenshots: `scratchpad/review/r1/`. Scripts: `r1/a.js`, `b.js`, `k.js`, `m.py`.

OCR results: balloon reads "THAT INPUT / ALREADY HAS / A WIRE!" and the toasts read "GRID SHOWN. / GRID HIDDEN." at all three widths, so the Anime Ace outlines hold up. Toast cap height measured: 11 px (1280), 12 px (1440), 17 px (1920). Balloon cap at zoom 1: 12-13 px (1440).

Contrast (computed): paper on --one 3.12:1 (live-row digits 35.8px/760, which counts as large text, so AA passes, but only just); ink on --one 6.06:1; --ink-2 on paper 4.54:1; ink on --caption 17.2:1; --grid on paper 1.30:1.

## Polish-pass verdict

**Not ready to ship yet. It will be ready after a short fix round and Tony signs off the flagged decisions.** The visual system is disciplined and specific: it does not look AI-made. Two findings block (R1-1 and R1-2: keyboard reach for truth-table rows, and the palette tab sitting last in tab order), and a group of quality findings are behaviour-level, not taste: click-added parts pile onto the demo gate, a rejection balloon never leaves, and the truth table parks a half-row under its sticky header. None of the fixes touches a locked decision.

Counts: 2 blockers, 10 quality, 12 polish. There is 1 DECISION FOR TONY.

## Findings

### Pass 1: ai-slop-check

**R1-20.** Spacing is off-scale. Current: fitted u-values are used as spacing: 53, 29, 26, 18.3, 12, 7, 5 (theme.css:63, 169, 97, 125, 253). Proposed: keep the fitted type metrics, but snap the *spacing* (padding, margin, gap, offsets) to 4u/8u multiples, or to the 20u canvas module the toasts already use. Why: ai-slop rule 8 and Müller-Brockmann: a single module governs intervals. Confidence 0.5. Severity polish. Screenshot: r1/base-1440.png

**R1-21.** Hover uses an invented colour. Current: `.disk:hover { background:#000 }` (theme.css:125). Measured, the difference from #111 is invisible; only the added ring registers. Proposed: keep `var(--ink)`, keep the ring, and drop the raw hex. Why: rule 7 (every colour traces to a token). Confidence 0.9. Severity polish. Screenshot: r1/1440-08c-disk-hover.png

**R1-22.** Row hover has a stray fallback colour. Current: `var(--grid, #f2f2f2)` (theme.css:295). The fallback is a seventh grey that is never used. Proposed: `var(--grid)`. Why: rule 7. Confidence 0.8. Severity polish.

**R1-23.** Pure white paper. Current: --paper `#ffffff` with ink `#111`. Proposed: nothing. The palette is a locked decision and the ink is already toned, so I'm noting it only because rule 6 asks. Confidence 0.3. Severity polish.

No gradient, emoji, left-border-card or illustration tropes were found. The editorial-warm check does not apply: cream appears only on toasts, as locked.

### Pass 2: hierarchy-rhythm-review

**R1-6.** Parts added by click pile onto the demo AND. Current: App.jsx:124-127 drops click/Enter adds at the canvas centre ±0-80 px. At 1440 that centre *is* the demo AND gate, so 4 switches and a lamp stacked on the AND and hid it (measured: overlapping bboxes at x 458-640, y 448-620). Proposed: place each new part at the first free 20u grid cell near the palette edge (left column below the switches), or run a collision check against the view. Why: position and density; a new part that hides an existing one reverses the hierarchy. Confidence 0.95. Severity quality. Screenshots: r1/1440-09-many-switches.png, 1440-11-zoomed-in.png, 1920-09-many-switches.png

**R1-8.** The truth-table block drifts vertically. Current: `.truth{justify-content:center}` (theme.css:271). The TRUTH TABLE label top moves from 318 px (4 rows) to 290 px (scrolling table) at 1440, and from 440 to 390 at 1920. Proposed: pin the label to a fixed row line: `justify-content:flex-start` with a top padding equal to the 4-row centred position, so the label never moves. Why: Swiss grid fixed-field placement. A heading that jumps when data changes breaks rhythm. Confidence 0.8. Severity quality. Screenshots: r1/base-1440.png vs r1/1440-10-table-scrolled.png

**R1-12.** Row 03 is empty. Current: `.status` and `.help` are always empty (App.jsx:302-304; "silent success, ref3 leaves row 03 empty"). That is 60u of framed band with only "03" in it. Proposed: DECISION FOR TONY. Either give the band one job, such as live counts ("3 PARTS · 2 WIRES · 2 INPUTS") set in the 16.9u help face, or drop the band and give its height to row 02. An NYCTA sign never ships an empty panel. Why: the hierarchy 5-second test (the empty band reads as "something failed to load") and Swiss economy. Confidence 0.6. Severity quality.

**R1-13.** The canvas ranks 4th. Current: wordmark ink is ~700x294 px at 1440, and the demo circuit's parts are 64 px wide. The first fixation lands on Figur, the disk and the table; the circuit reads as small line art in an empty field. Proposed: fit-view with less padding so the demo circuit fills about 60% of the canvas width (it spans about 90-890 of 960 now, but at 64 px per part it looks sparse), or raise the default zoom about 1.25x. Why: hierarchy by size, since the work surface should be the loudest thing after the brand. Confidence 0.5. Severity polish. Screenshot: r1/base-1920.png

**R1-14.** Row number 01 is off the wordmark baseline. Current: "01" baseline y=237 and Figur baseline y=245 (1440); 211 vs 218 (1280); 317 vs 328 (1920). The offset is a constant 7-8u. Proposed: set `.r1 .rownum{bottom}` so the 01 baseline sits on the F baseline (about 12u instead of 20u). Why: Müller-Brockmann baseline alignment; marginal numerals hang on the text line of their field. Confidence 0.6. Severity polish. Screenshot: r1/base-1440.png

**R1-15.** The palette tab straddles the gutter rule. Current: `.pal-tab` sits at left:-22 px, so at 1440 it spans x 33-77 across the ink rule at 53. When closed, half the tab sits in the row-number margin. Proposed: intended (it reads as a door hinge). Only confirm that it should also clear the row-number column at 1280, where the tab reaches x 27 and the "02" numeral ends at 38. Why: alignment. Confidence 0.3. Severity polish. Screenshot: r1/base-1280.png

**R1-16.** The balloon tail aims at the delete X, not the gate. Current: the tail tip ends at y=439 and the node's remove button starts at y=448 (1440). Both are ink-filled and 9 px apart, so the balloon seems to speak from the X. Proposed: hide `.remove` while the part holds a reject balloon, or move the tail tip 12u toward the body's optical centre. Why: hierarchy (a single speaker). Confidence 0.6. Severity polish. Screenshot: r1/1440-07-reject-balloon.png

**R1-17.** Header and body type sizes are near-duplicates. Current: th 32.4u/700 and td 35.8u/760 (560 off-row) differ by 10%. Proposed: stay inside the lock. The case for Tony: either equalise them (one size, weight carries the difference) or open the gap to at least 1.25x. Why: rhythm rule 2 ("identical or deliberately different"). Confidence 0.4. Severity polish.

**R1-18.** The balloon clips at the row-02 rule when zoomed. Current: at 2x zoom the reject balloon's top is cut by the canvas cell's overflow at y≈262, under the g descender. Proposed: flip the balloon below the part when its top would cross the cell top. Why: alignment and care. Confidence 0.5. Severity polish. Screenshot: r1/1440-11-zoomed-in.png

### Pass 3: polish-pass (accessibility + interaction states)

**R1-1. BLOCKER.** Truth-table rows are mouse-only. Current: `<tr onClick>` (Truth.jsx:77), row `tabIndex` measured at -1, no key handler, no role. Tabbing never reaches a row. Proposed: make each row a real control (a `<button>` in the first cell spanning the row, or `tabindex=0 role="button"` plus Enter/Space), with the same 3px ink focus ring used on the canvas. Why: WCAG 2.1.1 and accessibility-audit agent 3. Confidence 0.95. Severity blocker. Screenshot: r1/kb-15.png (focus falls to BODY after the canvas)

**R1-2. BLOCKER.** The palette tab is last in tab order. Current: Tab sequence disk, then 12 canvas stops, then the Open parts tab (stop 14), then body. The palette is the first thing a new user needs and sits at the far left, but it is rendered after `<ReactFlow>` (App.jsx:297). Proposed: render the Palette before ReactFlow in the DOM (visual position unchanged), or add a skip link "Add parts". Why: logical tab order follows reading order (agent 3.2). Confidence 0.85. Severity blocker (on the edge of quality). Screenshots: r1/kb-14.png, kb-01.png

**R1-4.** The reject balloon never leaves. Current: `reject` state clears only on a successful connect or a delete (App.jsx:147-152, 191). Measured: still on screen after 12 s, after grid toggles, after adding parts, after zooming. Proposed: auto-dismiss after the toast time (4 s), and on the next pointerdown on the canvas. Keep role=alert. Why: interaction-states pass phase 4, since feedback must not become stale state. Confidence 0.9. Severity quality. Screenshots: r1/1440-07b-after-reject.png, 1440-10-table-scrolled.png, 1920-09-many-switches.png

**R1-3.** The truth table parks a half-row under the sticky header. Current: after a programmatic or wheel scroll the first body row is cut. At 1440 the row "1 1 1 0" shows digits from y 397-425 jammed against the header rule. At 1280 the same happens, with the grey hover sitting on the half-row. The comment at Truth.jsx:52 promises "never half a row", but `scroll-snap-type: both proximity` (theme.css:294) does not enforce it. Proposed: `scroll-snap-type: y mandatory` on the vertical axis, with `scroll-snap-stop: always` or rounding in the onScroll handler. Keep x on proximity. Why: polish and alignment; a cut digit reads as a render bug. Confidence 0.85. Severity quality. Screenshots: r1/1440-11-zoomed-in.png, 1280-10c-table-end.png

**R1-5.** The picked output port looks the same as a focused port. Current: `.picked` is a 3px ink outline at offset 1 (theme.css:239), and `:focus-visible` is a 3px ink outline at offset 2 (theme.css:252). Once you Tab to the target input the picked state disappears into the ordinary ring, and the only pending cue is an sr-only status line. Proposed: give picked a distinct, persistent mark: ink fill in the knob with a paper ring (the pal-tab inversion idiom), or a dashed 6-4 wire stub following focus. Why: interaction-states phase 4 (current state visible). Confidence 0.8. Severity quality. Screenshot: r1/kb-montage.png (panels 2-3)

**R1-7.** No visible feedback when the palette adds a part. Current: click or Enter on a palette item adds silently at the centre (see R1-6). With the palette open over the canvas, the new part often lands under the bar or on another part. Proposed: select and focus the new node (the 2px dashed selection outline already exists), so keyboard users land on it. Why: interaction-states phase 4. Confidence 0.75. Severity quality.

**R1-9.** Row hover sits on a half-visible row, and the hover tint is too faint. Current: the hover tint is --grid #e2e2e2 on paper, 1.30:1. Proposed: acceptable for hover (non-essential), but pair it with R1-3. Why: agent 1 on difficult combinations. Confidence 0.4. Severity polish. Screenshot: r1/1280-10c-table-end.png

**R1-10.** The live row sits on the AA floor. Current: paper on --one is 3.12:1, which passes only because the digits are 35.8u/760 (large). The off-row weight of 560 does not apply to the live row, and at 1280 the digits are 31.8 px, still large. Proposed: no change, but never set lettered or small text in paper on --one (for example, a future live-row caption). Why: WCAG 1.4.3 large-text rule. Confidence 0.6. Severity polish.

**R1-11.** Node delete buttons are keyboard-unreachable. Current: `.node .remove{display:none}` until `:hover` (theme.css:220-223). `display:none` removes it from the tab order, so the `:focus-visible` rule can never fire. Delete/Backspace on a selected node works, but nothing tells you so. Proposed: `opacity:0` (or clip) instead of `display:none` so the button is focusable; alternatively, announce "Delete to remove" in the node's aria-describedby. Why: agent 3.1 (hover-only controls). Confidence 0.8. Severity quality.

**R1-19.** Reduced motion is only partly respected. Current: under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, `.pal-bar` still transitions at 0.16s and `.pal-tab` at 0.16s (measured). Only the toast animation is removed (theme.css:369). Proposed: add `.pal-bar, .pal-tab { transition: none }` to the same media block. Why: accessibility agent 4.1 (short, so low risk, but it's a slide). Confidence 0.7. Severity polish.

**R1-24.** The toast is announced twice and the stack is always live. Current: `.toasts` is role=status and each `.toast` holds an sr span, so toggling the grid twice queues "Grid shown" and "Grid hidden" together, and the stale first toast stays visible for 4 s as a contradiction (see screenshot). Proposed: when a new toast has the same topic as an existing one, replace it instead of stacking. Why: interaction feedback must reflect current state. Confidence 0.7. Severity quality. Screenshot: r1/1440-08b-grid-toast2.png

**R1-25.** Toast lettering is small at 1280. Current: cap height 11 px at 1280 and 12 px at 1440; OCR still passes. Proposed: floor the toast scale the way the other labels are floored (`max(u, f)`), so the caps never fall below about 12 px. Why: legibility across widths. Confidence 0.5. Severity polish. Screenshot: r1/1280-08b-grid-toast2.png

**R1-26.** The palette clips a half glyph at the bottom. Current: when the palette is open at 1440, 1280 and 1920, the NAND glyph shows only its top 6-8 px above the down-cue. The mask fades it, but the cut still reads as clipping. Proposed: size the list so a whole item or whole group boundary lands at the fold, for example by snapping the list height to 60u rows. Why: alignment and care. Confidence 0.4. Severity polish. Screenshot: r1/1440-02-palette-hover.png

## DECISION FOR TONY
- **R1-12:** the empty row-03 band. Give it one job (live counts) or remove it. The lock covers where toasts sit, not this band.
